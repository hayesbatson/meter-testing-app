Meter Testing App – Terms of use

The Meter Testing App is an internal tool built for and used only by Mid America Meter, Inc. ("MAM") employees to record water meter test results and prepare estimates. It is not offered to the public or to other companies.

QuickBooks Online connection

When MAM connects its own QuickBooks Online company, the app creates and updates estimates and reads customers and products & services in that company, only at the request of a signed-in MAM employee. MAM can disconnect the app at any time from QuickBooks (Settings → Apps) or from the app.

Use

Only MAM employees signed in with a MAM Microsoft 365 account may use the app. The app is provided as is, and MAM is responsible for reviewing estimates before they are sent to customers.

Contact

Mid America Meter, Inc. – questions about this app can be sent to MAM's office.
