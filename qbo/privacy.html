Meter Testing App – Privacy policy

The Meter Testing App is an internal tool used only by Mid America Meter, Inc. ("MAM").

What data the app uses

  • Meter test records, job details and customer contact and address details, stored in MAM's own Microsoft 365 (SharePoint) site.
  • From MAM's QuickBooks Online company: customer names and addresses, products & services, and the estimates the app creates.

Where it is kept

Records stay in MAM's Microsoft 365 tenant. The QuickBooks connection's access tokens are stored encrypted by the app's small service on Cloudflare and are used only to reach MAM's own QuickBooks company. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who can see it

Only MAM employees signed in with a MAM Microsoft 365 account.

Removing the connection

MAM can disconnect QuickBooks at any time; the stored tokens are then deleted.
